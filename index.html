StatusMitra privacy policy

App: StatusMitra: Family Wishes (com.reliableitstudio.statusmitra) · Developer: ReliableITStudio · Last updated: October 11, 2026

StatusMitra helps you make greeting cards for your family's everyday moments and celebrations. We built it to keep your personal information on your phone.

What stays on your phone

  • Your profile and preferences: your name, language, the people you send wishes to, your style and the celebrations you choose. These are stored privately on your device.
  • Cards and drafts: stored privately on your device.
  • Photos you add: copied into the app's private storage. StatusMitra never uploads them.
  • Voice notes: recorded only when you tap record, after Android asks for microphone permission. They stay on your device unless you share them.
  • Reminders: opt-in, and scheduled on your device.

StatusMitra has no accounts, no servers that receive your content, and no analytics. Android cloud backup is turned off for StatusMitra, so this data is not copied to your Google backup. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What leaves your phone, and why

  • Sharing: a card, text or voice note leaves your phone only when you choose Android's share action and pick an app. That app's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Celebration artwork: artwork for the celebrations you choose is delivered by the Google Play Store app (Play Asset Delivery). Google handles that download.
  • Purchases and billing country: StatusMitra uses Google Play Billing. From Google Play, StatusMitra receives only:
    • your Google Play billing country, used only to decide whether ads or the free trial apply. It never selects a language, culture or faith;
    • the local price of StatusMitra Plus, to show it to you;
    • whether you own StatusMitra Plus (a purchase status and token).
    Google handles your payment details; StatusMitra never sees them. The billing country and purchase status are also kept on your device so the app works offline.
  • Ads (Google Play billing country India only, unless you buy StatusMitra Plus): a small banner from Google Mobile Ads appears on the Wishes and Celebrations lists.
    • The ads are not personalized. Google states that non-personalized ads "still use cookies or mobile ad identifiers for frequency capping and aggregated ad reporting" (AdMob ad serving modes).
    • According to Google's data disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads SDK, the SDK collects and shares with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:
      • your IP address, which can estimate your general location;
      • app interactions such as launches and taps;
      • diagnostic information;
      • device identifiers such as the advertising ID.
      This data is encrypted in transit and handled under Google's privacy policy.
    • Outside India, and for anyone who owns StatusMitra Plus, StatusMitra never initializes Google Mobile Ads and never requests an ad. Parts of Google's ads library are still included in the app.
    • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Free trial

Outside India, celebration cards, voice notes and video sharing are free for 7 days, starting from the first time you use one. Nothing is charged automatically. The trial's start time is stored only on your device.

Children

StatusMitra is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

  • You can choose or remove celebrations, photos, voice notes and reminders at any time in the app.
  • Microphone and notification permissions can be turned off in Android settings.
  • Buying StatusMitra Plus removes ads in India.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stores.

Changes

If the app's handling of data changes, this page will be updated and the date above changed.
